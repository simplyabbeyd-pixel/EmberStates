## Intended use

Use this collection as a principal visual and 3D morphology reference for:

- creature type and elemental patterning
- body plans, limb arrangements, posture, locomotion, and silhouette
- anatomy and speculative biology
- proportions and comparative size mapping
- non-human design baselines for post-human or beyond-human EmberStates work

The design direction is to stop treating the ordinary human body as the default measuring template. Human measurements may still be used as temporary conversion anchors, but they do not control the final morphology.

## Evidence labels

Every extracted claim should be marked as one of:

- `directly_observed` — visible in the model or supplied screenshots
- `source_metadata` — stated on the model or collection page
- `canonical_reference` — supported by a separately cited authoritative source
- `user_assertion` — directly declared by Zarea
- `inferred` — reasoned from visible structure
- `speculative` — biological or worldbuilding interpretation

## Size mapping protocol

Record at minimum:

1. model page URL and creator
2. measured model-unit height, length, or wingspan
3. chosen reference dimension
4. meters per model unit
5. confidence level
6. whether the number is canonical, observed, estimated, or speculative

Do not force all creatures onto one universal human scale. Preserve multiple scale modes when useful: canonical scale, ecological scale, encounter scale, and avatar embodiment scale.
